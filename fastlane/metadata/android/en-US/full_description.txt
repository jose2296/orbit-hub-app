One space for each part of your life, and inside it whatever that part needs.

SPACES
A space is a place: Home, Work, the garage, whatever. Each one with its own colour and folders. Spaces are not global folders, so the shopping list does not get mixed in with work ones even when both are called "to do".

TASK LISTS
Tasks with an icon, a priority and an order. The icon matters more than it looks: a row that says "bread" and one that says "light bulb" can be read at a glance without reading them, and an emoji does not work because it looks different on every phone.

FILM, SERIES AND BOOK CATALOGUE
Search the catalogue and add to a list. The list keeps the poster, the year and which service it came from, not just some text. Then order them however you want: by year, by title, by when you added them.

NOTES
Notes with formatting, not plain text. With lists inside if you need them, with attachments, with templates for the things you write over and over.

SHARE
Share a note, a list or a folder with whoever you choose and decide whether they can view it or edit it. Invitations are answered inside the app, without opening the email.

WORKS OFFLINE
This is what you notice most on the underground. The app keeps what you see on the device, writes there first and syncs when you have coverage again. If there is no network you lose nothing: it syncs later.

ON MORE THAN ONE DEVICE
Android, iOS and web with the same account. What you write on the phone shows up in the browser and the other way round.

NO ADS
No ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases. The app is free and it does not sell your data.
